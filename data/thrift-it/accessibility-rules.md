# Digital Accessibility Standards (AR)

**Document ID:** DOC-AR-001  
**Version:** 1.4.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-08-01  
**Owner:** Accessibility & Quality Assurance Lead  
**Notice:** Fictional company policy for a demo. Regulatory references are illustrative, not legal advice.

---

[AR-000] Purpose and precedence. This policy guarantees inclusive access for all digital audiences, assistive technology users, and screen readers. Accessibility rules hold higher authority than Brand Voice (BV) and Visual Identity (VI). Visual aesthetics must yield where necessary to ensure full accessibility.

[AR-001] Mandatory image alternative text. Every published image must include alternative text (`alt_text`). Empty or placeholder alt text is a critical barrier for screen-reader users and will block publication.

[AR-002] Alternative text length specification. The alternative text must be between 20 and 125 characters in length. It must succinctly describe the featured garment, color, fit, and visual setting without redundant prefixing such as "image of".

[AR-003] CamelCase formatting for all hashtags. All hashtags must be written in CamelCase (capitalizing the first letter of each compound word, e.g., `#PreLovedFashion` or `#ThriftIt`) so screen readers parse words individually rather than pronouncing them as garbled phonetic strings.

[AR-004] Maximum hashtag count and positioning. A post may contain a maximum of 8 hashtags. All hashtags must be placed together at the very end of the caption or in a distinct footer section rather than sprinkled throughout body paragraphs.

[AR-005] Minimum WCAG AA text contrast ratio. Text rendered over solid background fills or photographic overlays must satisfy the WCAG 2.1 Level AA minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for normal text and 3.0:1 for large text. (For instance, Marigold `#F2A900` text on Paper `#FAF6EE` provides only ~1.7:1 contrast and is strictly prohibited).

[AR-006] Emoji usage restrictions mid-sentence. Emojis must not replace semantic words within sentences (e.g., writing "grab this 👕 for ₹299"). Screen readers read the full unicode description, disrupting comprehension. Emojis must be placed at the end of thoughts or sentences.

[AR-007] Information conveyance independent of color. Information, status indicators, size availability, or alert states must never be conveyed through color alone. Visual cues must always be accompanied by text labels or iconography.

[AR-008] Closed captions for video and reels. Any video clip, Instagram Reel, or audio asset containing spoken dialogue or voice-over must incorporate synchronized open or closed text captions.

[AR-009] Plain and readable language. Body copy should use plain, scannable sentence structures and avoid convoluted jargon or excessive decorative unicode fonts (such as bold or cursive script characters) that break screen-reader speech synthesizers.

[AR-010] Tap targets and interactive touch zones. Interactive digital elements, swipe prompts, and sticker links in stories must maintain a minimum touch target size of 44x44 CSS pixels with sufficient clearance from edge boundaries.
