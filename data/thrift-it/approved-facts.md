[AF-000] Purpose and precedence. This policy establishes the sole source of commercial, logistical, and promotional facts for Thrift-It. All statements regarding pricing, shipping, discounts, campaign dates, and store hours must match the fact registry. In hierarchy of precedence: Claims & legal > Approved facts > Social rules > Accessibility > Visual identity > Terminology > Brand voice.

[AF-001] Free shipping threshold and stale policy. Free shipping applies strictly to domestic orders above ₹999, effective 2026-08-01. Any reference to the obsolete ₹799 threshold (which expired 2026-07-31) is false and prohibited. Standard shipping fee for orders under ₹999 is ₹99.

[AF-002] Active commercial campaign: Diwali Thrift Fest. The current active seasonal promotion is the "Diwali Thrift Fest" running from 2026-10-20 to 2026-11-10. This campaign offers discounts of up to 30% off on curated festive collections.

[AF-003] Expired commercial campaign: Monsoon Clearance. The "Monsoon Clearance" offering flat 40% off concluded on 2026-09-15 and is now fully expired. Advertising or referencing this promotion is strictly prohibited.

[AF-004] Approved category starting prices. Minimum starting prices for standard curated items are: Tees starting from ₹299; Jeans/Denim starting from ₹599; Jackets starting from ₹899; Ethnic sets starting from ₹799. Advertising prices below these thresholds (such as jeans for ₹299) is factually incorrect.

[AF-005] Return and exchange policy. Customers may request returns within 7 calendar days of delivery for fit or size issues only. Exchanges for store credit are preferred. Items explicitly marked as "Final Sale" or accessories are strictly non-returnable.

[AF-006] Payment methods and Cash on Delivery rules. Cash on Delivery (COD) is available on orders with a total value under ₹3,000, subject to an additional ₹49 verification fee. Unified Payments Interface (UPI), debit cards, and credit cards are accepted on all order values with zero payment surcharge.

[AF-007] Shipping timelines and metro delivery. Standard courier transit takes 3 to 6 business days for Tier-1 metro destinations (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune), and 5 to 9 business days for Tier-2 and Tier-3 regions. Thrift-It does not offer same-day, instant, or 24-hour delivery under any circumstances.

[AF-008] Taxes, pricing transparency, and size standards. All advertised prices include applicable Goods and Services Tax (GST). Garments are measured manually with bust/chest, waist, and length recorded in centimeters (cm) across S, M, L, and XL standard sizing.

[AF-009] Hand inspection and condition grading guarantee. Every garment in the catalog is physically hand-inspected in our Mumbai curation studio and assigned an A, B, or C condition rating before being photographed and cataloged.

[AF-010] Physical Bandra pop-up operating schedule. The weekend physical retail pop-up store in Bandra West, Mumbai operates exclusively Friday through Sunday from 12:00 PM to 8:00 PM IST. It remains closed Monday through Thursday.

[AF-011] Accepted payment instruments. All transactions at the Bandra pop-up and online accept UPI QR codes, debit/credit cards, and net banking with zero processing surcharge. COD orders online incur ₹49 fee.
