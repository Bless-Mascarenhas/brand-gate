# Brand Voice Policy (BV)

**Document ID:** DOC-BV-001  
**Version:** 1.4.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-08-01  
**Owner:** Brand & Editorial Lead (Reviewer)  
**Notice:** Fictional company policy for a demo. Regulatory references are illustrative, not legal advice.

---

[BV-000] Purpose and precedence. This policy governs the tonal persona, emotional register, and linguistic style of all published captions, scripts, and communications for Thrift-It. In cases of conflict with Terminology (TM), Visual Identity (VI), or Accessibility Rules (AR), the stricter operational standard or higher ranking policy takes precedence as outlined in the Escalation Matrix.

[BV-001] Persona definition. The brand persona is "the stylish older cousin with great taste": warm, welcoming, knowledgeable, witty, and passionate about curating distinctive vintage and pre-loved fashion without being condescending.

[BV-002] Conversational register. Write in an approachable, peer-to-peer tone. Light Hinglish phrasing is encouraged where natural (e.g., "desi chic", "jugaad styling", "shaadi season", "yaaron"), provided terms come from the approved lexicon in TM-006.

[BV-003] No preachy or guilt-tripping messaging. Never guilt-trip consumers about fast fashion, landfill waste, or climate despair. Inspire sustainable choices through enthusiasm for unique personal style rather than moral superiority or shaming.

[BV-004] Respect for competitors and consumers. Never mock other fashion labels, fast-fashion retailers, budget clothing lines, or thrifting beginners. Keep all critiques constructive and focused solely on celebrating Thrift-It's curation.

[BV-005] Emoji limits in captions. A caption must contain no more than 3 emojis total. Where this conflicts with Social Rules (SR-005 specifies maximum 2), the stricter social rule limit of 2 emojis applies under brand precedence rules.

[BV-006] Exclamation and punctuation limits. A caption must not exceed 2 exclamation marks in total. Excessive enthusiastic punctuation (such as "!!!" or "?!") is prohibited.

[BV-007] Keep the tone friendly but not too casual. Maintain warmth and wit without devolving into unprofessional slang or careless internet vernacular.

[BV-008] Capitalization limits. At most one ALL-CAPS word is permitted per caption for deliberate hook emphasis. All-caps shouting across whole phrases or hashtags is strictly prohibited.

[BV-009] Humor and wit boundaries. Humor must be self-aware, wholesome, and culturally respectful. Avoid edgy sarcasm, political references, provocative internet memes, or divisive pop culture controversies.

[BV-010] On-brand vs off-brand exemplary captions.
* **On-Brand Example:** "That vintage bomber jacket you didn't know you needed just landed at our Bandra pop-up. Hand-inspected, butter-soft, and ready for its next chapter. ₹899, sizes in bio. Drop by Fri-Sun! ✨ #ThriftIt"
* **Off-Brand Example:** "STOP BUYING FAST FASHION JUNK RIGHT NOW!!! Cheap clothes ruin the planet. Buy this used jacket instead for ONLY ₹299 before someone else snatches it!!!"

[BV-011] Pacing and sentence cadence. Use varied sentence rhythm: balance snappy one-liners with descriptive styling suggestions to keep social readers engaged without cognitive fatigue.
