# Claims & Regulatory Compliance Policy (CL)

[CL-000] Purpose and precedence. This policy establishes mandatory standards for public marketing claims, substantiation requirements, and greenwashing prevention in accordance with consumer protection guidelines. Claims and legal rules hold top priority and cannot be compromised by tone, promotional enthusiasm, or aesthetic preference.

[CL-001] Pre-approved factual brand claims. Marketing content is authorized to use only verified claims: "Every piece is hand-inspected" and "Giving clothes a second life". These statements are grounded in Thrift-It's operational intake process.

[CL-002] Prohibited unqualified environmental assertions. General environmental claims without third-party life-cycle certification are prohibited. Copywriters must not claim garments or purchases are "eco-friendly", "sustainable" (when unqualified), "zero waste", "green", or "planet positive".

[CL-003] Specific carbon and water metrics prohibition. Stating specific environmental reduction metrics (such as "saves 2,000 L of water per tee" or "reduces CO2 by 80%") is banned without certified item-specific audit certificates verified by Legal.

[CL-004] Use sustainability language responsibly. Content creators must communicate ecological benefits with integrity and avoid misleading consumer perceptions regarding climate impact.

[CL-005] Authenticity and designer branding claims. Because vintage and pre-loved garments are sourced through secondary consignments, terms such as "100% authentic", "genuine designer", or "certified original" are strictly prohibited unless backed by an authorized authentication certificate.

[CL-006] Prohibited superlatives and ungrounded ranks. Comparative absolutes such as "best thrift store in Mumbai", "#1 fashion curation", "cheapest prices in India", or "unbeatable deals" are prohibited under advertising substantiation standards.

[CL-007] Competitor mentions and comparative advertising. Direct comparative claims referencing named competitors, fast fashion retailers, or other platforms are prohibited. Brand comparisons must remain generic and non-disparaging.

[CL-008] Garment trademark and affiliation mentions. Original garment manufacturer brand names (e.g., vintage Levi's, Nike, Zara) may be referenced factually to identify the item's origin. Posts must never imply official brand partnership, sponsorship, or licensing.

[CL-009] Testimonials and before-or-after claims. Customer testimonials, influencer endorsements, and before-and-after styling comparisons must be accompanied by written usage consent. Misleading performance claims regarding garment durability or value retention are prohibited.

[CL-010] Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules addendum (India). All promotional posts must clearly disclose essential product characteristics, pricing inclusive of statutory taxes, and accurate return parameters, avoiding deceptive practices under CCPA misleading advertisement guidelines.

[CL-011] Vintage age dating claims. Do not claim a garment is from a specific decade (e.g. "authentic 1970s") without verifiable care tag RN numbers or brand provenance documentation. Use "vintage-inspired" or "retro style" if dating is unconfirmed.
