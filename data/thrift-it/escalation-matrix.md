# Governance & Escalation Matrix (EM)

**Document ID:** DOC-EM-001  
**Version:** 1.5.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-08-01  
**Owner:** Head of Brand Governance & Legal Counsel  
**Notice:** Fictional company policy for a demo. Regulatory references are illustrative, not legal advice.

---

[EM-000] Purpose and precedence. This matrix defines operational roles, review authority, override protocols, and escalation pathways for all brand governance issues. The strict hierarchy of governing precedence is: Claims & legal > Approved facts > Social rules > Accessibility > Visual identity > Terminology > Brand voice. In any conflict where precedence is silent, the stricter standard governs.

[EM-001] Creator self-resolution scope. Content Creators possess authority to draft posts and self-resolve minor terminology variations, typo corrections, safe hashtag adjustments, and light tonal refinements without requiring managerial sign-off.

[EM-002] Managerial approval requirements. Any post triggering major findings, pricing discrepancies, commercial offer validations, or substantive claim alterations requires formal review and written approval by a Manager role before moving to ready state.

[EM-003] Legal and disclosure dual approval. Content with critical severity findings, influencer paid-partnership ambiguities, ASCI disclosure questions, or potential trademark liabilities requires dual concurrence from both the Legal Reviewer and the Marketing Manager.

[EM-004] Visual identity governance authority. Deviations regarding color palettes, wordmark spacing, photographic filter grading, or aspect ratios require explicit review by the Brand Designer (Reviewer role).

[EM-005] Resolution of guideline conflicts and ambiguity. When two governing clauses appear to conflict or when multi-model debate generates recurring unresolved disputes, the issue must be escalated to the Manager to author an official policy clarification or amendment.

[EM-006] Mandatory human publishing decision. The Brand Gate platform and its AI components shall never automatically publish content to external social networks. A human reviewer holding the appropriate role authority must make the final affirmative publishing decision.

[EM-007] Protocol for critical finding overrides. A critical gate finding or verified critical violation can be overridden only by a user acting in the Manager role. The override requires a mandatory typed rationale of at least 20 characters, which is permanently logged in the audit trail.

[EM-008] Role privilege boundaries and simulations. For the purposes of this demo environment, roles are selectable via the top navigation bar. In enterprise deployment, roles are strictly mapped to verified SSO identity claims (Creator, Manager, Legal/Brand Reviewer).

[EM-009] Immutable audit logging requirement. Every transition through review states (drafting, automated check, managerial override, approval, simulated publish) must generate an immutable audit receipt containing the timestamp, user role, model trace, and active policy pack hash.

[EM-010] Routine policy calibration and review cycle. Governing documents and fact registries must be reviewed monthly. Stale promotional campaigns and expired seasonal discounts must be marked as expired promptly to prevent automated publication drift.
