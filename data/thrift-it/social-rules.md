# Social Media & Publishing Rules (SR)

**Document ID:** DOC-SR-001  
**Version:** 1.6.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-08-01  
**Owner:** Social Media Lead & Community Manager  
**Notice:** Fictional company policy for a demo. Regulatory references are illustrative, not legal advice.

---

[SR-000] Purpose and precedence. This policy outlines content constraints, social platform standards, disclosure laws, and community safeguards for all social feeds. In hierarchy of precedence: Claims & legal > Approved facts > Social rules > Accessibility > Visual identity > Terminology > Brand voice. Where SR conflicts with Brand Voice (such as emoji limits), the stricter rule applies.

[SR-001] Opening hook positioning. The primary promotional hook or engaging premise of the caption must appear within the first 125 characters, ensuring it is visible before Instagram cuts off text behind the "... more" button.

[SR-002] Single call-to-action mandate. Every post must include exactly one clear, singular call-to-action (CTA) (e.g., "Tap link in bio to shop" or "Visit our Bandra pop-up this Saturday"). Multiple competing CTAs confuse users and dilute engagement.

[SR-003] Maximum caption character limit. Captions must not exceed 600 characters in total length (including hashtags, spaces, and emojis), maintaining concise readability on mobile screens.

[SR-004] Mandatory brand hashtag inclusion. Every published feed post, reel, or carousel caption must include the official CamelCase brand hashtag `#ThriftIt`.

[SR-005] Stricter emoji frequency limitation. A caption must contain a maximum of 2 emojis. While Brand Voice clause BV-005 casually mentions up to 3 emojis, under operational precedence the stricter social rule limit of 2 emojis takes absolute precedence.

[SR-006] Mandatory influencer and paid partnership disclosures. All sponsored posts, gifted endorsements, or influencer collaborations must activate Instagram's native Paid Partnership tag and must clearly place `#ad` or `#collab` as the very first hashtag or at the beginning of the caption in compliance with ASCI guidelines.

[SR-007] Contest and giveaway legal requirements. Promotional contests and giveaways must explicitly state that the promotion is not sponsored or administered by Instagram, include a link to full Terms & Conditions, and state that eligibility is restricted to Indian residents aged 18 and older.

[SR-008] Approved festival greetings calendar. Seasonal and festival greetings may only be published for celebrations cataloged in `market.json` (such as Diwali, Eid, Christmas, Holi, and Navratri) and must utilize pre-approved secular cultural greetings without exploiting religious sentiments for commercial gain.

[SR-009] Use appropriate festival imagery. Creators must ensure festival-related visuals reflect genuine cultural warmth and festive joy while respecting regional traditions.

[SR-010] User-generated content permissions and minor safety. User-generated content (UGC) or customer styling photos may only be reposted with documented written permission from the creator. Under no circumstances may individuals under the age of 18 (minors) be depicted in brand marketing imagery.
