# Terminology & Approved Lexicon Policy (TM)

**Document ID:** DOC-TM-001  
**Version:** 1.3.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-08-01  
**Owner:** Copy Lead & Managing Editor  
**Notice:** Fictional company policy for a demo. Regulatory references are illustrative, not legal advice.

---

[TM-000] Purpose and precedence. This policy establishes the required vocabulary, prohibited terms, brand spelling rules, and approved bilingual Hinglish expressions for Thrift-It. Terminology rules are enforced deterministically by the gate engine. In any conflict with Brand Voice (BV), TM takes precedence.

[TM-001] Preferred descriptors for garments. When describing items in inventory, copywriters must use approved positive descriptors: "pre-loved", "thrifted", "curated", "archival", "second life", "vintage find", or "handpicked".

[TM-002] Prohibited garment descriptors. The following terms are strictly banned in all customer-facing text: "cheap", "junk", "old clothes", "second-hand" (in captions; permitted only in alt text or hashtags), and "used" as a garment quality descriptor.

[TM-003] Conditions for newness claims. The descriptors "brand new" and "unworn" are prohibited unless accompanied by explicit verification that original manufacturer tags remain fully intact (e.g., "deadstock with intact tags").

[TM-004] Official brand name capitalization and formatting. The brand name must always be spelled and formatted as "Thrift-It" with a hyphen and capitalized "T" and "I" in all body copy and headlines. Forms such as "ThriftIt", "thrift-it", "Thrift-it", or "Thrift it" are strictly prohibited. The official brand hashtag must always be formatted in CamelCase as `#ThriftIt`.

[TM-005] Product category naming conventions. Standard product categories must strictly adhere to capitalized title conventions: "Tees", "Denim", "Jackets", "Ethnic", "Accessories", and "Knitwear". Generic informal titles like "T-shirts", "pants", or "desi kurtas" must map to their official category names.

[TM-006] Approved Hinglish vocabulary. The following Hinglish words are approved for creative captions: "desi", "jugaad", "shaadi", "yaaron", "bambai", "bindass", "dhamaka", "jhakaas", "chashma", "kurtis", "dupatta", and "thodi".

[TM-007] Prohibited slang and derogatory terms. Informal street slang that detracts from a curated, premium experience is banned, including: "chindi", "kachra", "ghanta", "faltu", "bakwaas", "chapri", "sasta", "loot lo", and "bhasad".

[TM-008] Pricing terminology. State prices with the Indian Rupee symbol "₹" followed immediately by numerals without a space (e.g., "₹599"). Do not use "Rs.", "INR", or "bucks" in public marketing copy. Always clarify that prices are inclusive of GST.

[TM-009] Condition grading vocabulary. The condition of garments must be referenced according to official hand-inspection grades: "Grade A" (like new / pristine), "Grade B" (minor signs of pre-loved charm), or "Grade C" (visible distress or vintage character).

[TM-010] Sustainability and circularity vocabulary. Use precise circularity phrases such as "keeping garments in circulation" or "giving garments a second life". Never use ungrounded corporate jargon such as "zero waste footprint" or "planet-saving purchase".

[TM-011] Measurement and sizing terminology. Express dimensions consistently in centimeters ("cm") and chest/waist measurements rather than subjective terms like "fits oversized" without reference numbers.
