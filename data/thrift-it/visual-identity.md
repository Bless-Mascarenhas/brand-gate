# Visual Identity & Design Guidelines (VI)

**Document ID:** DOC-VI-001  
**Version:** 1.5.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-08-01  
**Owner:** Brand Designer (Reviewer)  
**Notice:** Fictional company policy for a demo. Regulatory references are illustrative, not legal advice.

---

[VI-000] Purpose and precedence. This policy governs color palettes, wordmark usage, typography, image aspect ratios, and visual styling across all graphic and photographic assets for Thrift-It. In any direct visual conflict with Accessibility Rules (AR), accessibility contrast takes legal precedence.

[VI-001] Approved brand color palette. The approved color palette comprises exactly five hex codes: Thrift Teal (`#0E7C7B`), Marigold (`#F2A900`), Ink (`#1B1B1F`), Paper (`#FAF6EE`), and Chili (`#D7263D`, reserved exclusively for urgent status alerts). Neon or unapproved pastels are strictly disallowed.

[VI-002] Official wordmark lockup and clear space. The brand wordmark consists of "Thrift-It" with the letterforms set in Ink or Paper and the hyphen specifically filled in Marigold (`#F2A900`). Clear space around the wordmark must equal at least the height of the capital letter "T". Never alter proportions, skew, or apply drop shadows.

[VI-003] Wordmark placement on photographic backgrounds. When placing the wordmark on complex or high-frequency photographic backgrounds, it must be enclosed within a solid Paper (`#FAF6EE`) or Ink (`#1B1B1F`) background band to guarantee legibility.

[VI-004] Call-to-action button and banner styling. Call-to-action (CTA) banners and buttons must feature a Marigold (`#F2A900`) background fill with Ink (`#1B1B1F`) text. Note: Setting Marigold text on Paper backgrounds yields a contrast ratio of ~1.7:1, which severely violates accessibility standards (AR-005 requires 4.5:1 minimum); in accordance with brand precedence, Marigold text on Paper is strictly prohibited.

[VI-005] Social media image aspect ratio formats. Visual assets must strictly conform to standard Instagram format dimensions: Feed Portrait 4:5 (1080x1350 px), Feed Square 1:1 (1080x1080 px), or Story/Reel 9:16 (1080x1920 px). Off-aspect crops or distorted scaling are rejected.

[VI-006] Image text overlay coverage limit. Graphic overlays, promotional badges, and typography must not occupy more than 25% of the total image surface area, preserving photographic focus.

[VI-007] Photographic lighting and styling. Product and editorial photography must utilize natural daylight or soft ambient lighting. Harsh artificial flashes, high-contrast neon lighting, and heavy saturated Instagram filters are prohibited.

[VI-008] Authentic backdrops and realistic settings. Photography must showcase real Mumbai streetscapes, curated studio corners, or neutral minimalist interiors. Generic, artificial studio backdrops or AI-generated fantasy backgrounds are prohibited.

[VI-009] Model casting and realistic representation. Imagery must feature diverse everyday body shapes and genuine personalities. Hyper-edited stock glamour models or heavily airbrushed portraits are prohibited.

[VI-010] Prohibition of third-party watermarks. Uploaded photographs must be completely devoid of third-party agency watermarks, competitor logos, stock agency stamps, or foreign platform badges.
